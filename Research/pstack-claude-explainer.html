Name a goal. poteto-mode ships only verified work.

pstack routes your request to one of 23 playbooks, hands each step to background subagents, and ships nothing until a check on the real artifact passes.

How it routes

A hook decides who handles the task; poteto-mode decides how

pstack is plain Markdown that the agent reads. There is no server. A hook injects one rule at session start, and poteto-mode’s SKILL.md carries the rest: triggers, principles, the playbook list and model defaults.

Playbooks

23 playbooks, each a fixed recipe with a gate it will not skip

A playbook is a Markdown file of numbered steps. poteto-mode copies the steps word for word into the agent’s todo list. A step it skips stays in the list with skip: <reason>. Most playbooks end with Opening a PR.

Skills

Most heavy skills fan out to subagents, then one synthesizer decides

Playbooks call skills. A skill is one SKILL.md the agent reads and follows. The multi-agent ones share one shape: several independent views, then one merge step that you can audit.
